# Shed

## Context
The garden shed is too small for the tools, and the mower stays outside in the rain.

## Objective
* Every tool has a dry place.
* The mower is under a roof.

## Decision
1. **Build a lean-to** on the north wall of the shed, two metres deep.
2. **Move the shelves** from the shed to the lean-to.
3. **Keep the mower** in the shed, where the shelves were.
4. **Ask the town** about planning permission before buying wood.

## Consequences
* The shed needs a wider door.
* Tools are sorted by use when the shelves move.
